## THE LEAD

SpaceX plans a $24 billion gas-fired power plant and super-compute facility on 444.45 acres near the Port of Brownsville in South Texas, according to a city of Starbase notice issued Monday. The plant could power the data center without connecting to the state grid, where Gov. Greg Abbott asked operators in August to pause new data center connection requests. Brownsville de-annexed the land this year at SpaceX's request after SpaceX offered $220 million for water and wastewater projects, and SpaceX pressed the city to approve quickly or lose the money. Brownsville's 90-day data center moratorium began last week, and the Texas Tribune reported the project likely would have needed another site had the land stayed in the city. Starbase commissioners plan a December hearing on tax breaks. The South Texas Environmental Justice Network announced a protest citing air pollution, water use and higher energy bills. Janie Melendez, a Brownsville teacher who backs the project for jobs, said, "Since SpaceX moved in, my home has tripled in value."

> The full story: [Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/10/texas-spacex-data-center-power-plant/)
