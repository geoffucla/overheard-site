## THE LEAD

Dell Technologies, based in Round Rock, Texas, reported record third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Nov. 24, with revenue of $54.9 billion, up 103% year over year. Diluted earnings per share were $8.82, and operating cash flow reached a record $4.5 billion. The Infrastructure Solutions Group drove the quarter, with revenue up 198% to $42.0 billion, including $22.0 billion from AI-optimized servers. PC revenue rose 3% to $12.9 billion. The AI backlog ended the quarter at $132.3 billion after $60.2 billion in new orders. Dell raised its full-year revenue outlook to $202.0 billion from $192.0 billion and forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $56.3 billion. It returned a record $4.9 billion to shareholders, and its board approved an additional $25.0 billion repurchase authorization. Non-GAAP operating expenses fell to 7% of revenue, the lowest in Dell's 42-year history, according to David Kennedy. The figures are Dell's own. For Texas workers, suppliers and investors tied to Dell, the backlog shows how much of the company's business now rests on AI server orders.

## THE LEDGER

~ Money in, money out, receipts attached.

CrowdStrike, $43.9 billion, Acquisition. Austin-based CrowdStrike sells cybersecurity software, and Zscaler sells network security. CrowdStrike agreed Nov. 23 to buy Zscaler in an all-stock deal valued at about $43.9 billion in enterprise value. Each Zscaler share converts to 1.135 CrowdStrike shares, about $260.41 a share and about a 34% premium to the Nov. 20 close. Zscaler holders would own about 15.9%, and the deal should close in the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal 2028, subject to approvals. (Source: [CrowdStrike SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001535527/000153552726000035/crwd-20261123xex991.htm))

Mount Street, $1.4 billion in assets, Servicing book acquisition. Mount Street, a London-based loan servicer with a U.S. arm in Atlanta, is opening a Dallas hub by hiring a team from Dallas firms Broadsword Investors and Goldersun Loan Servicing and acquiring Goldersun's servicing book of almost $1.4 billion in assets, which joins a roughly $30 billion U.S. book. Rawle Howard, head of Mount Street USA, said the team "marks a meaningful acceleration of Mount Street's U.S. growth strategy." (Source: [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/capital-markets/mount-street-dallas-hub-paul-smyth-greystone-broadsword))

## LOCAL DESK

~ Texas, close to home.

ADP announced Nov. 19 that it has acquired Austin-based ZenBusiness, which helps people start businesses. The deal is complete, and ADP did not say what it paid. ZenBusiness CEO Ross Buhrdorf said, "I can't imagine a better home for our team, our clients and our mission."

> Deal notes: [Investing News](https://investingnews.com/adp-acquires-zenbusiness/)

Texas new-home sales fell to 5,018 in October, the lowest of 2026 and down 7.6% from October 2025, according to a HomesUSA.com release. Dallas-Fort Worth had 1,657 sales, the most pending contracts at 1,878 and the longest time on market of the four metros at 130 days. CEO Ben Caballero said pending sales "are at their lowest levels since 2022" outside San Antonio.
