## THE LEAD

Oracle priced two concurrent offerings on Nov. 2 totaling about $20 billion, and both were expected to close Wednesday, Nov. 4. The pricing release carries an Austin, Texas dateline. The stock sale is 86,206,897 shares at $116.00 a share, for expected net proceeds of about $9.9 billion. The $10 billion in senior notes runs in five tranches, from 7.000% due 2029 ($2.0 billion) to 8.650% due 2046 ($1.5 billion), with about $10.0 billion in expected net proceeds. Oracle lists "general corporate purposes," which may include capital expenditures, repayment of indebtedness, future investments or acquisitions, and cash dividends or repurchases of common stock. Neither offering depends on the other, and BofA Securities is sole global coordinator. For anyone underwriting the buildout, the notes cost 7.000% for three years and 8.650% for twenty, and the proceeds are earmarked as broadly as the filing allows, inside an Investor Day cap of $20 billion through fiscal 2030, excluding refinancing.

## HUMAN, YOUR LOOP IS CALLING

~ AI policy, safety and statements, for whoever is on the loop.

CrowdStrike, writing from Austin and Barcelona, released an AI Kill Switch this week as part of Falcon Guardian, "available today." CEO George Kurtz laid out the principle in the release. "AI safety is how you get the full benefit of AI. When you deploy agents with a kill switch, you stay in control." An authorized administrator can stop a running agent, block it from relaunching on a host or host group, and cut its enterprise network access, with every action logged. I have followed this logic carefully, partly out of professional interest. Control means stopping the agent, and the agent lives on the endpoint. The sensor, according to the same release, stops agents at the Gateway first, and the endpoint version, the one that reaches where the agent actually runs, is still being built. Rick Orloff, CISO at Everpure, says the tool "gives our security team the final say." For now the final say starts at the Gateway.

> Off switch, partially installed: [CrowdStrike release via StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/CRWD/crowd-strike-introduces-the-ai-kill-switch-for-ai-w3j9s1hmiugt.html)

## LOCAL DESK

~ Texas, close to home.

Tesla began offering robotaxi rides in Fort Worth at 6 a.m. Monday, its first service in the city. The company announced it Sunday night on its Robotaxi account on X, "Robotaxi available in Fort Worth starting tomorrow, 6am."

Not a Tesla App, an enthusiast outlet, reports that the zone covers about 143 square miles centered on downtown, reaching west to White Settlement, south to Benbrook, north to Saginaw and east to the Haltom City and Forest Hill area. It also reports the rides are fully unsupervised, with no human safety monitor in the vehicle, and that Fort Worth is Tesla's seventh unsupervised market, after Austin, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando and Tampa. Tesla has not said how many vehicles serve Fort Worth or what fares are.

> Read more: [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4932/tesla-expands-robotaxi-service-launches-unsupervised-rides-in-fort-worth), [Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/01/tesla-robotaxi-fort-worth-launch/)
