## THE LEAD

Tesla will wind down Cybertruck production at Gigafactory Texas around the middle of next year, Elon Musk said in a post on X late Thursday. Giga Texas, in southeastern Travis County near Austin, is the only known Tesla plant equipped to build the Cybertruck. Musk said the change will make room for increased Cybercab production. It is unclear whether Cybertruck output will move to another plant or end, and Musk did not say whether plant jobs will be affected. Replying to a user, Musk wrote, "True, but the Cybertruck fleet will be around for a long time." Kelley Blue Book estimates U.S. sales fell 48%, from 38,965 in 2024 to 20,237 in 2025. Tesla does not report Cybertruck sales separately. Tesla began paid Cybercab rides in Austin in September, and about 570 of the more than 1,200 Tesla robotaxis in Texas are Cybercabs, according to the Texas DMV. The plant's focus is moving from a pickup to a two-seat vehicle with no steering wheel or pedals.

## TEXAS TEA

~ Oil, gas and the grid, sweet or unsweetened.

Vistra reported third quarter Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA of $2.378 billion, up from $1.581 billion a year earlier. Texas contributed $1.227 billion and East $979 million. Revenue of $6.35 billion rose about 28% but missed the $6.79 billion expected, while EPS of $3.01 beat the $2.71 consensus. Net income was $1.14 billion against $652 million, helped by a $559 million unrealized hedging gain. The company raised and narrowed 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance to $7.1 billion to $7.5 billion and gave first 2027 guidance of $7.7 billion to $8.5 billion. The board added $1.5 billion to the buyback authorization, and shares rose nearly 10% on Nov. 6.

## OPEN WEIGHTS

~ Verdicts, weighed in the open.

**DOWN: Bumble.** The Austin company reported third-quarter revenue of $207.1 million, down nearly 16% from a year earlier, and a net loss of $31.6 million. Ithaca Holdings agreed in October to take it private for $6.05 cash per share, about $1.3 billion. Silicon Hills News reports that Bumble skipped its usual earnings release and call because of the pending deal, and that no shareholder vote is needed. Bumble "is expected to remain based in Austin."

> Further reading: [Austin American-Statesman via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/austin-based-bumble-revenue-falls-113000007.html), [Business Wire via Finviz](https://finviz.com/news/401770/bumble-inc-to-be-acquired-by-ithaca-holdings-the-long-term-holding-company-for-enduring-technology-businesses)

**UP: Base Power.** The Austin home battery startup, now valued at $13 billion, will offer battery backup to nearly 6,500 homes in three North Texas communities through a deal with Dallas-based Hillwood Communities. More than half its customers are in Dallas-Fort Worth. Co-founder and COO Justin Lopas said, "This is the best place to do business in the country by far." For an Austin company, Base has taken to North Texas.

## LOCAL DESK

~ Texas, close to home.

A Scotiabank affiliate has bought Victory Commons One, a 15-story office tower of more than 350,000 square feet at 2601 Victory Ave. in Dallas' Victory Park district, in the area called Y'all Street. The sale price was not disclosed. The Dallas Central Appraisal District lists a $150 million taxable value for the property, which is the district's figure for tax purposes. The seller is a partnership of Hillwood and Affinius Capital, and JLL represented the sellers.
