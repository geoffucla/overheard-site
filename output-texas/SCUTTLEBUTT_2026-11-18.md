## THE LEAD

The Texas Commission on Environmental Quality, the state's environmental regulator, has resumed reviewing data center permits, ending a pause Gov. Greg Abbott ordered in a Sept. 21 letter. TCEQ said Abbott's office notified it that ERCOT, the state grid operator, had finished gathering information on the projects' potential community impacts. Spokesperson Laura Lopez said the agency "has resumed its normal permit review process" and will apply all applicable state and federal laws. ERCOT told the Public Utility Commission of Texas on Nov. 5 that it had responses from 93% of 465 projects, and it plans to report full findings on Dec. 10. Public Citizen's Kathryn Guerra said TCEQ has not shared results or explained how they would factor into permits, and the group called on commissioners to resign. Abbott lost reelection Nov. 3 to Democrat Gina Hinojosa, who vowed to keep approvals paused until legislators pass new laws. Permits for data center construction and operation now move again before ERCOT's findings are public.

## LOCAL DESK

~ Texas, close to home.

AT&T and developer KDC broke ground in the week before Nov. 16 on a 54-acre campus at 5400 Legacy Drive in Plano. Seven buildings totaling 2.2 million square feet are planned, along with a 250-foot cell tower, with move-in expected in the second half of 2028. No dollar figure was disclosed. CEO John Stankey said, "Our new headquarters in Plano is an investment for our current and future employees, and our North Texas community."
