## THE LEAD

4Minds, a Dallas enterprise AI startup, raised a $15 million Series A at a $500 million post-money valuation, according to a report on Nov. 12. The investors are Dallas-area family offices rather than venture capital firms. Named backers are Waterwood Capital Group of Dallas and Isom Holdings of Lubbock, along with family offices tied to the energy and data center industries. Founded in 2024, the company came out of stealth in April. Its platform connects more than 40 business systems and runs inside the customer's own environment. Headcount was 13 in February and is now above 60, with nearly 100 expected by early 2027. The money will go to sales, marketing and product, and the company declined to give revenue targets. CEO Ryan Trimberger said, "We think we can scale and be in the nine-figure range very quickly." The round shows Texas family money can fund an enterprise AI company at this valuation without a venture firm.

> The full story: [Dallas Innovates](https://dallasinnovates.com/dallas-ai-startup-4minds-raises-15m-series-a-at-500m-valuation/)

## THE LEDGER

~ Money in, money out, receipts attached.

Fervo Energy, $11.9 billion, Q3 results. Fervo, a Houston geothermal developer listed on Nasdaq as FRVO, reported on Nov. 9 that contracted revenue rose to about $11.9 billion from $7.2 billion, on 1,054 megawatts of binding power purchase agreements. Third-quarter revenue was $116,000, the operating loss $43.7 million and unrestricted cash $1.9 billion. Cape Station in Utah reached first power and commercial operation. (Source: [Energy Capital](https://energycapitalhtx.com/fervo-backlog-q3-earnings))

Hoque Global, $96 million, Incentive withdrawn. Hoque Global is a Dallas developer behind Newpark, a 20-acre, $450 million downtown district. The city withdrew the incentive agreement it approved in 2022, saying the developer missed financing and permit deadlines. CEO Mike Hoque still intends to build, and says high interest rates make a hotel unviable without an active convention center, whose $3.7 billion overhaul now opens in 2030. (Source: [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/dallas-ft-worth/development/hoque-global-s-450m-downtown-dallas-project-loses-city-incentive))

## TEXAS TEA

~ Oil, gas and the grid, sweet or unsweetened.

The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission has issued a construction permit for four 80-MW X-energy Xe-100 reactor units at Dow's Seadrift Operations on the Gulf Coast between Houston and Corpus Christi, to be known as the Long Mott Generating Station. NRC Chairman Ho K. Nieh said the agency "delivered on the 18-month schedule." The power is intended for onsite industrial users such as petrochemical plants and data centers, and construction could begin before 2030. Diane Wilson of San Antonio Bay Waterkeeper called the project "outrageous". The NRC dismissed legal challenges filed in 2025. X-energy has supported the Texas Tribune financially. Dow has not yet made a final decision to proceed.

> The full story: [Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/12/nuclear-texas-dow-x-energy-permit-reactors-seadrift/)

## LOCAL DESK

~ Texas, close to home.

Watter, a Dallas startup spun out of Hunt Innovative Technologies, is running its first site at the Hyatt Regency Dallas at Reunion Tower. One rack of 64 Nvidia GPUs captures the heat from AI computing to supply the hotel's hot water, and the hotel could earn over $100K next year. Watter wants five more DFW sites within a year. The honest answer, if a guest asks why the water is so hot, is that a computer has been thinking very hard.

> Read more: [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/dallas-ft-worth/data-center-power/dallas-based-startup-aims-to-turn-ai-waste-heat-into-hot-showers-for-hotel-guests)

Toyota's Plano-based North American engineering arm received a patent for a self-leveling camping air mattress that "uses a series of air chambers and sensors to give the user a flat surface on which to sleep." It took 564 days to grant, the fastest of seven Toyota patents that week.

> The full story: [Dallas Innovates](https://dallasinnovates.com/patented-toyotas-self-leveling-air-mattress-and-122-more-inventions-across-north-texas/)

## CORRECTIONS AND UPDATES

~ Where we were wrong, or the story moved.

On Nov. 9 we reported Gina Hinojosa's account that a SpaceX land item in the Boca Chica area was pulled from a Texas Parks and Wildlife agenda at her request. On Nov. 12 SpaceX confirmed it has discussed buying about 1,200 acres of public land near Starbase and said it would preserve 600 acres as coastal habitat. Starbase administrator Cayetana Polanco said "nobody is buying or selling Boca Chica Beach." The commission did not address the item in November. So far only the agenda item has been cleared.

> The ground shifts: [Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/12/spacex-boca-chica-park-details/), [Earlier edition](https://tx.termsundisclosed.com/editions/2026-11-09/)
