## THE LEAD

Gov. Greg Abbott said Monday that a reported sale of Boca Chica State Park land to SpaceX is "not coming up for a vote anytime soon." The park sits in Cameron County beside SpaceX's Starbase near Brownsville, and all nine Texas Parks and Wildlife commissioners are appointed by Abbott. A department spokesperson said the commission is not considering the sale at its November meeting. Texas Monthly reported Oct. 16 that a draft agenda listed a closed-session discussion of "1,300 acres" in Cameron County for Nov. 5, and two unnamed sources told the magazine the buyer is SpaceX. Abbott said he knew little about the details and wants to preserve Texas parkland. The Texas Tribune puts the park at more than 1,055 acres, a gap left unexplained, and no price has been reported. SpaceX did not immediately comment. SpaceX's launch site depends on land it does not own, and the state park is the beachfront beside it.

## THE LEDGER

~ The numbers, kept as plainly as possible.

Flex, $4.4 billion, Acquisition. Austin-based Flex completed its purchase of California-based EPC Power on Oct. 14. The deal, announced Sept. 3, was to be funded by debt and equity. EPC Power expects about $800 million of 2026 revenue. Flex plans to spin off the unit's parent segment as Axiom Solutions International in the first calendar quarter of 2027. (Source: [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000866374/000119312526382492/d108491dex991.htm))

TotalEnergies, $1.2 billion, Joint venture expansion. TotalEnergies, U.S.-headquartered in Houston, said Oct. 15 it is adding three solar assets in Texas and Ohio totaling 700 MW to its 50/50 venture with KKR. The venture grows from 1.4 GW to 2.1 GW. Closing is expected this year. (Source: [Energy Capital](https://energycapitalhtx.com/totalenergies-expands-solar-joint-venture))

Hertha Metals, $133.65 million, Series A. The Conroe iron producer announced the round Oct. 6, co-led by Khosla Ventures and Doerr Capital. It includes $65 million of equity from the Pentagon. Proceeds fund a high-purity iron plant producing 10,000 metric tons a year, with groundbreaking planned this year. (Source: [Energy Capital](https://energycapitalhtx.com/hertha-metals-series-a))

Third Coast Bancshares, $239.6 million, Acquisition. Houston-based Third Coast agreed Oct. 7 to buy Oklahoma City's Great Plains Bancshares in an all-stock deal. The combined bank would hold about $9 billion in assets and 43 branches. Closing is expected in the first quarter of 2027. (Source: [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/third-coast-texas-buy-oklahoma-bank-240-million/832392/))

## TEXAS TEA

~ Oil, gas and the grid, steeped plainly.

Texas upstream oil and gas employment stood at 196,400 in September, down 500 from a revised 196,900 in August and up 2,400 from September 2025. Extraction lost 100 jobs and services lost 400. Employment fell in four of the first eight months of 2026, with a February low of 191,500 and a May peak of 197,300. The count covers extraction and support activities and excludes refining, petrochemicals, pipelines and equipment manufacturing. TIPRO counted 12,719 unique industry job postings in Texas in September, against 3,688 in California, the next highest. TIPRO president Ed Longanecker said, "Producers are operating with greater efficiency even as costs remain elevated." The EIA's October outlook, as cited by TIPRO, forecasts record U.S. crude output of 13.87 million barrels a day in 2026, with growth driven mainly by Texas.

## OPEN WEIGHTS

~ Verdicts, weighed in the open.

**UP: Citi's Irving inventors.** Five of the six inventors on a Citibank patent granted Sept. 22 are from Irving. It covers a large language model that drafts the suspicious activity reports filed with FinCEN once a fraud risk score crosses a threshold. The patent notes that as few as 4% of those filings warrant a follow-up from law enforcement. The patent office took 666 days to approve it, so the paperwork, at least, is consistent.

> The patent, summarized: [Dallas Innovates](https://dallasinnovates.com/?p=293103)

**UP: Apple's Texas supply chain.** From Sherman, Coherent supplies the lasers behind Face ID and the Dynamic Island on the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. Apple's Advanced Manufacturing Center in Houston, opened in August, builds AI servers and will start making Mac mini this year. Spread the whole $600 billion four-year U.S. commitment across that 20,000-square-foot Houston floor and it comes to $30 million a square foot, so wipe your feet.

> The full release: [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-announces-american-manufacturing-program-partners-for-latest-iphone-lineup/)

**DOWN: Texas's clean-air record.** Between 2002 and 2024 Texas sharply cut major air pollutants while its economy roughly doubled. A Houston Advanced Research Center report has statewide nitrogen oxide emissions up 35% by 2031 with grid power plus on-site renewables, and nearly 400% with on-site gas. In the gas scenario volatile organic compounds would top 2002 levels by 32%, which turns the starting line of the cleanup into the good old days.

> Breathe in the details: [Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/20/data-centers-texas-air-quality-report-pollution-health-risks/)

## AUTOMATIC REPLIES
