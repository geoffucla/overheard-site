## THE LEAD

Archer Aviation on Friday announced plans for electric air taxi networks in Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth and Houston, using its Midnight aircraft. The Dallas-Fort Worth network would cover six airports, including DFW International and Dallas Love Field, plus Frisco Station. Houston would have three airports and Austin three sites. These are announced plans from the company, and none of it is an operating service. Archer has not announced a launch date, and no regulatory approval for the individual routes has been confirmed. Archer says flights would cut 60 to 90 minute drives to 10 to 20 minutes, a claim that has not been independently verified. Charging infrastructure is still being built, and a consortium including Archer is working with the Texas Department of Transportation on charging in Texas. The FAA released draft vertical-flight infrastructure standards Oct. 22, with comments due Jan. 11, 2027. The plans give the three largest Texas metro areas a test of how quickly air taxi infrastructure can be built and approved.
