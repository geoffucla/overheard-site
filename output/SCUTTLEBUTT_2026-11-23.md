## THE LEAD

CrowdStrike agreed Monday to buy Zscaler, the Santa Clara security company, in an all-stock deal with an enterprise value of about $43.9 billion. CrowdStrike's release carries an Austin dateline and Zscaler's carries Santa Clara. Zscaler holders would receive 1.135 CrowdStrike shares per share, an implied $260.41 and a premium of about 34%. CrowdStrike holders would own about 84.1% of the combined company and Zscaler holders about 15.9%. Founder Jay Chaudhry and affiliates, who hold about 34% of Zscaler shares, agreed to support the deal, and he joins the CrowdStrike board. The companies target $1 billion in revenue synergies within 30 months and $500 million in cost synergies within 18 to 24 months. Combined ARR would top $10 billion, with $3.9 billion of it from Zscaler. Chaudhry said, "AI agents are rapidly becoming the new weakest link." Closing is expected in the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal 2028.

## OPEN WEIGHTS

~ Verdicts, weighed in the open.

**UP: Signal.** Signal now limits screenshots on Android and iOS, a default it already runs on Windows, after Meredith Whittaker called agentic AI built into operating systems an "existential threat." She said those operating systems, including Android from Mountain View and iOS from Cupertino, "are undermining Signal's ability to independently guarantee privacy." Users can switch the restriction off, which Whittaker says would "break our privacy guarantees."

> Read more: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/21/signal-restricts-mobile-screenshots-as-it-confronts-existential-threat-of-ai-surveillance/)

**UP: Gemini on the Mac.** Google's Gemini Mac app can now operate the computer itself, working in the background while a live preview lets the user pause, stop or approve each move. It is open to US users in English on a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra plan, and TechCrunch judges Google "a little late to the game." The pause, stop and approve controls are the part that keeps a human in the loop.

> Exhibit B: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/google-gives-gemini-the-ability-to-use-your-mac-for-you/)
