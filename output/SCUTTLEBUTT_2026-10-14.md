## THE LEAD

Federal prosecutors have charged a former OpenAI engineer with hiding an audio recording device in an executive conference room in San Francisco. Abdulrahman Alfozan, 35, a Saudi national, was arrested Oct. 9 and appeared in federal court Oct. 13. He is charged with using, or attempting to use, a device to intercept oral communications. Staff found the device around Sept. 30 in a USB port on the back of a television, hidden in toilet paper, its contents encrypted. Prosecutors allege that on Oct. 5 he entered the room and appeared to look behind the television, where OpenAI had placed a camera, and left a bag in another room holding a second similar device. OpenAI fired him and alerted the FBI. It said it has "no indications of this individual inappropriately accessing or exfiltrating model weights." The charge carries up to five years in prison and a $250,000 fine. Alfozan has not been convicted. U.S. Attorney Craig H. Missakian said, "For us it is a matter of national security."
