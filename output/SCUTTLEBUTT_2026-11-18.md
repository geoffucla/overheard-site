## THE LEAD

California's Department of Motor Vehicles has ordered an end to the driverless freeway operations of Waymo, which runs robotaxis in San Francisco and on the Peninsula, saying they "pose an imminent hazard to public safety." The order took effect Nov. 11 and became public Nov. 17. The order follows two incidents. Three Waymos entered an active construction closure on US 101 near Burlingame, and a separate episode on I-280 in San Francisco involved California Highway Patrol vehicles. Waymo has stopped freeway access in all of its markets, which span 15 US cities. Surface-street service continues, with more than half a million trips each week. Freeway service was suspended once before, in May, and resumed in July. SFO service has now been lost a second time. Spokesperson Dana Gallagher said "Safety is at the foundation of everything we do."

> The full story: [SFist](https://sfist.com/2026/11/17/waymos-get-kicked-off-california-freeways-again-after-viral-incident-on-i-280/)

Investors include Benchmark, Felicis, General Catalyst, Sapphire, NVIDIA, Salesforce, Zoom and Apollo. (Source: [Ventureburn](https://ventureburn.com/mercor-raises-750-million-series-d-to-train-ai/))

Amazon Web Services, $5 billion, Sale-leaseback. Seattle-based AWS is selling a 75% interest in data center campuses in Indiana and Mississippi, for about $5 billion, to Blackstone's BXDC joint venture and leasing them back for 12 years on a triple net lease covering 647 MW. Closing is set for Q4 2026, with a $150 million termination fee, and it is Amazon's first such deal. (Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2026/11/17/reit-move-blackstone-joint-venture-buys-aws-campuses-to-lease-back-to-the-cloud-giant/5310355))

## OPEN WEIGHTS

~ Verdicts, weighed in the open.

**UP: Google.** Google would like it known that Gmail is fine. After a Downdetector rumor of a Gmail outage began to circulate, the company went to X and posted that Gmail was working normally, and TechCrunch's headline said Google "joins the tech outage trend by announcing it's not having one."

> Reported by: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/google-joins-the-tech-outage-trend-by-announcing-its-not-having-one/)

**DOWN: Optimizely.** Its Web Experimentation product became the way in for attackers on Monday, Nov. 16, and the damage spread to some of the largest sites online. Injected JavaScript reached at least 178 organizations, including the BBC, CNN, Zoom, IKEA and Salesforce, and 188 of the 1,611 scripts examined were malicious, carrying a crypto clipper and a card skimmer. About $7,000 in crypto was traced. Optimizely said "an unauthorized party exploited a vulnerability in Experimentation."

> Detailed by: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/11/17/optimizely-hack-turns-worlds-biggest-websites-into-malware-dispensers/5310448)

## LOCAL DESK

~ The Bay Area, close to home.

The Pax Silica San Francisco Declaration was signed Nov. 16 at a retreat in San Francisco convened by Under Secretary Jacob Helberg. The United States signed alongside Australia, India, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Qatar, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and the United Kingdom. Germany, the Netherlands and Sweden are expected to join. The declaration includes five working groups. The signing came against a backdrop of data center moratoriums in San Francisco. Separately, tens of thousands of people protested a massive AI hub in the Philippines.

## YOUR CALL IS IMPORTANT TO US

~ News from the other end of the support line.

On Nov. 17, iCloud sign-in stopped working for "all users," and the App Store and Apple Music went down with it. Service was back by 3 PM PT. Apple told TechCrunch the cause was an internal update and that it was not a cyberattack. The same day, Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton told Fox Business, "We do not see the indicia of a coordinated cyberattack," and pointed to businesses patching, possibly after AI found vulnerabilities. The standard advice for anyone who cannot sign in to an account is to make sure their software is up to date.

> On the outage: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/apple-services-including-icloud-app-store-and-apple-music-hit-by-outage/)
