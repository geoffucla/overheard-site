## THE LEAD

Meta disclosed in an SEC filing last week a deal meant to keep CEO Mark Zuckerberg in California, where the company is headquartered in Menlo Park. The filing says Zuckerberg told a special board committee he was "evaluating relocating his primary residence outside California" as Prop 40, a one-time tax of up to 5% on billionaire wealth, heads to the Nov. 3 ballot. His exposure is about $12 billion. Paying could have forced him to sell Class B supervoting shares, cutting his voting control from about 60.6% to 53%. Meta's board approved a new class of nonvoting Class C shares, to be issued in January 2027, one for each existing Class A or Class B share. He may initially sell 20% of his Class C shares, with 2.5% more eligible each quarter while he stays in a full-time leadership role. In return he committed to remain a California resident for eight years. Sales of nonvoting stock could fund a tax bill without touching his control.

## LOCAL DESK

~ The Bay Area, close to home.

San Francisco's $10 million sales no longer cluster in Pacific Heights. A "mansion shortage" has pushed north-side sales routinely above $20 million and brought $10 million sales to Mission Bay and Hayes Valley, near AI job centers. Compass says September listings were the fewest since 2020, while sales above $5 million rose 109% year over year. A home at 1125 Hayes listed at a $7 million teaser price sold for $9 million within days.
